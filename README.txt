BUSINESS ECONOMICS — SEMESTER 1 MASTER STUDY & EXAM PORTAL

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Do not move index.html out of the folder.
2. Open index.html with Chrome, Edge, Firefox or another modern browser.
3. The portal is a static website; no Python, Node, backend, login or database is required. Bootstrap and Google Fonts are optional CDN resources retained from the original site; local CSS provides the layout when they are unavailable. All content, SVG images, question data and interactions are stored locally.
4. You can also serve the directory with a local web server or upload all files to a static hosting service such as GitHub Pages.

CONTENTS
- Five expanded modules with terminology, Indian classroom examples, tables, formulas and stories.
- Exactly 39 original prompts arranged into five modules and grouped by 2, 5 and 10 marks. Each has 2-, 5- and 10-mark model-answer variants (117 answers). The original marks are the default answer depth. The 2- and 5-mark answers follow the source limits of 50 and 150 words.
- 17 standalone worked numerical problems and 16 exercises (12 instant-check exercises and four original practice items), plus worked examples throughout the notes.
- 17 labelled static graphs and 7 vector concept illustrations, and 4 dynamic slider labs.
- 10 case-study scenarios (six new solved workbook scenarios and four original discussion scenarios).
- Five module-wise descriptive practice sessions with module-dependent marks and optional countdown, response fields, word counters and model-answer self-assessment. All questions in each module are available to attempt.
- Five module-wise concept quizzes (39 total) with instant answer explanations.
- Exam writing strategies and final revision flashcards, formulas and comparison tables.

SOURCE ALIGNMENT AND LIMITATIONS
The five-module sequence follows the uploaded website. All 39 question prompts, CO and BTL codes were verified against the three supplied Alliance Global Business School Semester End Examination DOCX files. A separate full syllabus or official marking scheme was not supplied in this turn; added explanations cover topics in the existing site and papers. Examples, exchange rates, graphs, questions for extra practice, model answers and suggested marking guidance are original illustrative teaching material, not official answers or future-paper predictions. Descriptive answers require self-assessment; they are not automatically graded by the website. Browser responses reset on reload or when a new module is chosen. No personal information is sent to a server.
